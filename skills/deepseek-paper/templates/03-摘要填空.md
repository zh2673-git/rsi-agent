# 摘要填空表（T 类母版）

> 用法：逐槽填。填不出的槽 = 证据缺口，回账本补；不许用定性词糊过去。
> 目标：150–180 词，6–8 句。

## 槽位

| 槽 | 内容 | 取值规则 | 例（V3 原文） |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 开篇 | `We present <Model>, a <架构类型> language model with <N> total parameters, of which <M> are activated for each token.` | 参数量必须成对 | `We present DeepSeek-V3, a strong Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language model with 671B total parameters with 37B activated for each token.` |
| S2 目标从句 | `To achieve <目标A> and <目标B>, <Model> adopts <已验证技术> and pioneers <新机制>.` | `adopt` 只用于上一代已发表件；`pioneers` 只给一个 | `To achieve efficient inference and cost-effective training, DeepSeek-V3 adopts Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA) and DeepSeekMoE architectures … and pioneers an auxiliary-loss-free strategy for load balancing` |
| S3 效率对比 | `Compared with <基线>, it achieves significantly stronger performance, and meanwhile saves <pct1>% of training costs, reduces the KV cache by <pct2>%, and boosts the maximum generation throughput to <k> times.` | 三个百分比都要有 | `… saves 42.5% of training costs, reduces the KV cache by 93.3%, and boosts the maximum generation throughput to 5.76 times.` |
| S4 主结果 | `Comprehensive evaluations reveal that <Model> outperforms other open-source models and achieves performance comparable to leading closed-source models, achieving <s1> on <B1> and <s2> on <B2>.` | ≥2 个带分 benchmark | `achieving 88.5 on MMLU, 75.9 on MMLU-Pro, and 59.1 on GPQA` |
| S5 成本 | `Despite its excellent performance, <Model> requires only <X>M H800 GPU hours for its full training.` | 硬件型号 + 量级 | `2.788M H800 GPU hours` |
| S6 稳定性（可选） | `In addition, its training process is remarkably stable. Throughout the entire training process, we did not experience any irrecoverable loss spikes or perform any rollbacks.` | 只在真有此事实时写 | 同左 |
| S7 开源/链接 | `The model checkpoints are available at <URL>.` | 无开源则写 `We release …` 或删掉此槽 | `https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V3` |
| S8 归因（M 类用） | `The <能力> of <Model> is attributed to two key factors: First, … Second, …` | 两因素写法 | Math 摘要 |

## 另外三种开篇范式（体裁不同就换）

- **架构创新（问题先行）**：`In the era of …, X is … However, <痛点>. In response, we propose <机制> towards <目标>.`（MoE）
- **领域攻坚（挑战先行）**：`<任务> poses a significant challenge for language models due to <原因>. In this paper, we introduce <Model>, which …`（Math）
- **系列迭代（introduce 型）**：`We introduce our <第 N> 代 <类别>, <Name-A> and <Name-B>.`（R1）

## 自检
- [ ] 每句都有数字或机制名，没有纯形容词句
- [ ] `pioneers` 只出现一次
- [ ] 至少 2 个 `<分数> on <benchmark>`
- [ ] 末句是链接或开源声明
- [ ] 全文无 `novel` / `to the best of our knowledge` / 裸 `state-of-the-art`
