# 03 措辞指纹：动词纪律、句式、禁用清单

> 用途：这是「像不像」的决定性一层。写完全文后逐条过。
> 全部例句为 DeepSeek 原文摘录（arXiv HTML 页），`…` 表截断。

## 3.1 动词按「创新归属」选（最稳定的纪律）

| 动词 | 只能用于 | 原文 |
|---|---|---|
| **pioneer** | 自称首创、且是全文最卖点 | `DeepSeek-V3 pioneers an auxiliary-loss-free strategy for load balancing` |
| **propose** | 提出新架构/新算法（学术贡献） | `we propose the DeepSeekMoE architecture towards ultimate expert specialization` |
| **introduce** | 首次把某技术引入本体系、首次做某事 | `we introduce an FP8 mixed precision training framework and, for the first time, validate its effectiveness` |
| **design** | 自研工程件/调度/流程 | `we design the DualPipe algorithm for efficient pipeline parallelism` |
| **adopt** | 沿用他人/自家上一代已验证件 | `DeepSeek-V3 adopts Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA) and DeepSeekMoE architectures, which were thoroughly validated in DeepSeek-V2` |
| **present** | 「本文呈现」与图表引用 | `We present DeepSeek-V3, …` / `is presented in Figure 2` |
| **investigate / explore** | 探索性、结论未定 | `we investigate a Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) objective and prove it beneficial` |
| **employ / use** | 纯配置陈述 | `we employ the AdamW optimizer` |
| **observe / find** | 实验发现，不断言因果 | `which we have observed to enhance the overall performance` |
| **validate / demonstrate** | 结论强度分级 | `consistently validate its substantial advantages` |
| **recommend** | 对外部（尤其硬件厂商）提建议 | `we recommend that future chip designs increase accumulation precision in Tensor Cores` |

**最常犯的错**：把你从别处借来的东西写成 `propose`。凡是上一代论文/开源库里已有的，只能 `adopt`；只在工程实现上自研的，用 `design` 不用 `invent`。

## 3.2 形容词与限定语

- 高频：`remarkably`、`notably`、`consistently`、`significantly`、`substantial`、`impressive`、`meticulously`、`elegant`。
- 「非第一」的标准说法：**`top-tier performance among open-source models`**（V2 摘要、V2 结论、V3 摘要均用此语）。
- `state-of-the-art` **必须带范围限定**：`achieves state-of-the-art performance on math-related benchmarks among all non-long-CoT open-source and closed-source models`。裸用扣分。
- 「同规模」是必需限定语：`with only half of the activated parameters`／`with only about 40% of computations`／`even with only 21B activated parameters`。

## 3.3 一级句式（可直接套）

**目的从句开场**（最标志性）：
> `In order to achieve the best of both worlds, we introduce MLA, an attention mechanism equipped with low-rank key-value joint compression.`（V2）
> `To further push the boundaries of open-source model capabilities, we scale up our models and introduce DeepSeek-V3`（V3）

**同位语解释链**（定义术语的固定手法，`X, a/an <类别> that/which <作用>`）：
> `GRPO, an efficient and effective reinforcement learning algorithm`（Math）
> `DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA), an efficient attention mechanism that substantially reduces computational complexity`（V3.2）

**弱化断言**：`we observe that` / `we find that` / `as expected`。

**免责与边界句**（`Note that …` 单句成段）：
> `Note that the bias term is only used for routing.`
> `Note that the aforementioned costs include only the official training of DeepSeek-V3, excluding the costs associated with prior research and ablation experiments on architectures, algorithms, or data.`

**否认式缓冲**：`However, …` 起句 + `we also acknowledge that` / `it is worth noting that`。

## 3.4 连接词位置化使用

- 枚举 2–3 项机制：`Firstly, … Secondly, …`（V3 引言第 3、4 段）。
- 段内归因：`First, … Second, … Third, …`（Math 摘要两因素、R1 §4.2 对 PRM 的三条限制）。
- 超过 3 项 → 改用 bullet，不再堆序数词（硬规则）。
- 递进四选一轮换、同节内不重复：`Furthermore` / `In addition` / `Moreover` / `Additionally`。

## 3.5 实验句式

引用图表四选一轮换（`As shown in` / `As depicted in` / `As illustrated in` / `As presented in`）：
> `Figure 2 depicts the performance trajectory of DeepSeek-R1-Zero on the AIME 2024 benchmark throughout the RL training process. As illustrated, DeepSeek-R1-Zero demonstrates a steady and consistent enhancement in performance as the RL training advances.`

> `Table 2 provides a comparative analysis between DeepSeek-R1-Zero and OpenAI's o1-0912 models across a variety of reasoning-related benchmarks. The findings reveal that …`

对手逐个点评用 `(1)…(2)…(3)…` + `Compared with X, …`，**必须主动承认劣势**：
> `(3) Compared with LLaMA-3.1 405B Base, the largest open-source model with 11 times the activated parameters, DeepSeek-V3-Base also exhibits much better performance on multilingual, code, and math benchmarks. As for English and Chinese language benchmarks, DeepSeek-V3-Base shows competitive or better performance`
> `While it trails behind GPT-4o and Claude-Sonnet-3.5 in English factual knowledge (SimpleQA), it surpasses these models in Chinese factual knowledge (Chinese SimpleQA)`

幅度写法：`surpasses existing open-source base models by over 10% absolute`／`outperforming 96.3% human participants`／`jumping from an initial 15.6% to an impressive 71.0%`。

成本口径澄清句（DeepSeek 特色，必须跟一句）：
> `Note that the aforementioned costs include only the official training …`

## 3.6 数字的四条硬规则

1. 参数量成对：`<N> total parameters, of which <M> are activated for each token`（禁止只说「激活参数 M」）。
2. 相对提升写「动作 + 具体百分比」：`saves 42.5% of training costs, reduces the KV cache by 93.3%, and boosts the maximum generation throughput to 5.76 times`（V2）。
3. 成本给绝对值 + 硬件单位：`only 2.788M H800 GPU hours for its full training`；`$5.576M`；单价假设写进 caption：`assuming the rental price of H800 is $2 per GPU hour`。
4. benchmark 分内嵌句末：`achieving 88.5 on MMLU, 75.9 on MMLU-Pro, and 59.1 on GPQA`。

## 3.7 禁用清单（硬门禁）

| 禁用 | 为什么 | 替代 |
|---|---|---|
| `to the best of our knowledge` | 通读 V3/R1/V2/Math/MoE 全文未见 | 直接说 `we pioneer/introduce`，或写明范围限定 |
| `novel`（自夸形容词） | 同上 | `new` 不必写；用机制名本身说话 |
| `we argue that` / `we claim` | 同上 | `we observe` / `we find` / `we demonstrate` |
| `In this paper, we propose a novel …` 式开场套路 | 与 DeepSeek 摘要开篇范式冲突 | 见 `templates/03-摘要填空.md` |
| 裸 `state-of-the-art` | 无范围即失实 | `state-of-the-art … among <范围>` |
| `the author(s)` | 人称只用 `we`/`our` | `we` |
| `it is well known that` | 套话 | 删，或改成具体引文 |

（以上「未见」为弱证据：基于 V3/R1/V2/Math/MoE 的 HTML 全文通读，未做全库逐词检索。）
